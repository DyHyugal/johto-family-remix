# Pokédex & Locations

This section is intended to become the quick reference for one simple question:

> **“Where can I find this Pokémon in Family Remix?”**

## Entry format

The final version will use a compact table:

| Pokémon | Area | Method | Level | Time | Rarity |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Example | Route | Grass / Surf / Rod / Rock Smash | 00-00 | Day/Night/Any | Common/Rare |

When a Pokémon has several useful locations, they will be grouped into the same entry instead of split across many pages.

## Family Remix encounter philosophy

Distribution is built around progression:

- 4 main species per table;
- target weighting **30 / 30 / 30 / 10**;
- make strong use of the encounter methods available on each map;
- final evolutions mainly appear later;
- wild starters are rare and progressively unlocked;
- no Legendary/Mythical Pokémon are added as ordinary random encounters;
- Safari-designated species remain tied to Safari content.

## V1 data set

The final working data contains:

- **409 normal tables**;
- **53 Safari tables**;
- **462 total tables**.

### Johto Safari

Target level range: **37-44**.

### Kanto Safari

Target level range: **68-76**.

## Methods covered

Depending on the map, location entries can include:

- land/grass;
- Surf;
- Rock Smash;
- Old Rod;
- Good Rod;
- Super Rod;
- Day/Night variants where available;
- Safari;
- other Heart & Soul-specific encounter methods.
